FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still stuck? Ask on Discord.

Is Huistrap free?

Yes. Huistrap is free and open source. You can read every line of it on GitHub.

Windows shows a SmartScreen warning. What do I do?

New apps without a paid code-signing certificate often get this warning. Click “More info”, then “Run anyway”. If you'd rather check first, the full source is on GitHub.

Where are my accounts stored?

On your PC only. Saved logins are encrypted with your Windows account using Electron's safeStorage and never leave your machine.

Roblox broke after an update. Can I go back?

Yes. Open Settings → Deployment and press Roll back. Huistrap keeps the previous version after every update. If files are damaged, press Repair instead.

How does Huistrap itself update?

It checks for a new version on startup and shows Update Now on the home screen. Nothing installs until you click it.

Is this made by Roblox?

No. Huistrap is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Roblox Corporation.
